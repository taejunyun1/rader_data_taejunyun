How photography was reinvented 43 times
TPG
2026-06

A timeline built by Lev Manovich and Anthropic's Claude model that 'presents a history of the automation of image capturing, processing, editing, and audience — and how it led to the disappearance of the photographic image from photography.' The two present modes are with software/computation & generative automation. A core point about the present is that photography no longer has a 'bond to reality'.

Trust in the image becomes cryptographic — distinction between photograph and generated image moves from visual to metadata; the photograph must prove it is a photograph
